Golden Turmeric Dahl

35 min 4 servings
ATN312 present

A warming, anti-inflammatory bowl for cold evenings — gentle on digestion and deeply grounding.

N

Your guide

Noa Castell

Breathwork · Nourishment

Ingredients

1 cup red lentils, rinsed
1 tbsp fresh turmeric, grated
1 can coconut milk
2 cloves garlic, minced
Handful of spinach

Method

1
Soften garlic and ginger in a little oil until fragrant.
2
Add lentils, turmeric, and stock; simmer 20 minutes.
3
Stir through coconut milk and wilt in the spinach.
4
Finish with lime and serve warm.
