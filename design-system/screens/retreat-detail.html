A week of slow mornings, sea air, and somatic practice in a restored finca above the Tramuntana coast.

The Experience

For one week, twelve people share a table, a coastline, and the unhurried work of coming home to themselves.

Mallorca is our slowest gathering. Days are built around the breath and the body — long mornings of movement, afternoons left open for the sea or sleep, and evenings around a single long table. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is performed.

Who This Is For

  • Anyone carrying more than they've had space to put down.
  • People drawn to depth over spectacle — small, real, unhurried.
  • Those who want to be met as they are, not fixed.

If you've been waiting for permission to rest, consider this it.
