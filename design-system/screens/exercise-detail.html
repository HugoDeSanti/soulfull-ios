Standing Release for the Shoulders

A slow sequence to unclench the upper body after a long day at the desk. Move at the pace of your breath.

The Sequence

1
Stand tall, soften the knees, and let the arms hang heavy.
30 seconds
2
Roll the shoulders back and down, slow circles.
×8 each way
3
Reach one arm across the chest, breathe into the stretch.
45s per side
4
Interlace hands behind you, lift gently, open the heart.
3 breaths
5
Return to center, eyes closed, notice the difference.
1 minute
