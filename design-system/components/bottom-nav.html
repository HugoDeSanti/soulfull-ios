BottomNav — floating glass tab bar

Active = ink capsule + label + bounceIdle = icon only, white 0.85

Glass capsule floats 18 above the home indicator with an 0.28 drop shadow. Active tab grows an ink-filled lens (matched geometry) with its label and a .bounce.down symbol effect; spring (response 0.42, damping 0.72). Tabs: Home · Programs · Practice · You. The bar bows out (offset +140, fade) on detail pushes. The bar builds itself from MainTab.allCases, so a tab is added or removed by touching the enum alone — which is how Circle left and Programs arrived.
