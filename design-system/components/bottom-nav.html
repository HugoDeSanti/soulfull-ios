BottomNav — flat, docked tab bar

Active = ink icon + labelIdle = mutedYou = member's photo

Docked to the bottom edge, full width: Theme.bgDeep @ 0.94 with a 0.5pt Theme.line hairline on top. Six items — Home · Gatherings · Digital Retreats · Practice · Community · You — each as wide as its label plus an equal share of the spare width (NavBarLayout), so the gaps between labels are even and the slots still tile the whole bar. Equal slots stopped fitting at six: "Digital Retreats" is ~77pt against a ~64pt sixth. A light-weight glyph over a 10pt Geist Medium label; Theme.ink when selected, Theme.muted otherwise. Nothing animates: no lens, no bounce, no press dim; hiding on detail pushes is an instant fade-free cut. The You item is the member's AvatarView (22pt, a 1pt ink ring when selected — a face, so it stays round); a guest sees the plain person glyph. Builds from MainTab.allCases.
