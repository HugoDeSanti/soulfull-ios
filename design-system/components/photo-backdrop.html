Atmosphere · moss

PhotoBackdrop is only for screens where the photograph is the content — Welcome, onboarding, the retreat stack and detail: a Theme.bg floor → the image (saturation .9, blur ≤ 6, contained in a Color.clear overlay so scaledToFill can't inflate layout) → a top→bottom vignette → the top scrim. Every other screen sits on Atmosphere: black, with one tone falling from the top (moss · ember · dusk · slate), breathing slowly. A content photo is never blurred into a page's tint any more.
