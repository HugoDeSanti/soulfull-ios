Eyebrow = Theme.eyebrow(11), uppercase, positive tracking, Theme.inkSoft (ember only as a small mark). SectionLabel opens a section as a printed page does: a 0.5pt Theme.line rule across the measure, then 14pt below it the title in tracked caps in Theme.ink; the action ("ALL") in Theme.inkSoft, a 44pt target, fires Haptics.tap(). The size lives in the content below, not in the heading. VoiceOver reads the title as written, not spelled out in caps.
